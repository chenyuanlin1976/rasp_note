# FPC Antenna

Flexible Printed Circuit Antenna (FPC Antenna) is a type of internal antenna  
built on a flexible substrate—such as polyimide (PI) or polyester (PET)—designed  
for modern wireless communication and compact electronic devices.

Because of its thin, bendable, and high-performance profile, it is widely used across various industries where space is at a premium.

## Key Advantages

1. High Flexibility & Spatial Adaptation: Built on flexible materials, FPC antennas can be bent, folded,  
   or curved to fit tightly against the interior walls of a device's plastic casing,  
   making them ideal for space-constrained designs.
2. Easy Installation (Peel-and-Stick): Most FPC antennas come with a strong adhesive backing (such as 3M tape),  
   allowing them to be easily mounted inside the device enclosure and connected to the mainboard via a micro coaxial cable (like an IPEX/U.FL connector).
3. Lower Ground Plane Dependency: Unlike standard Surface-Mount Device (SMD) on-board antennas,  
   FPC antennas are less restricted by the size or layout of the host PCB's ground plane, offering greater design freedom.
4. Reliable RF Performance: They deliver stable omnidirectional radiation patterns and high radiation efficiency,  
   balancing aesthetics with solid wireless link quality.

## Common Applications

+ Consumer Electronics: Smartphones, laptops, and tablets for Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and GPS.
+ IoT & Smart Hardware: Wearable devices, smart meters, asset trackers, and wireless monitoring gear.
+ Automotive & Telematics: In-vehicle infotainment, navigation, and telematics modules.
+ Industrial & Drones: UAV remote controllers, video transmission systems, and robotics navigation.

## Typical Frequency Bands

FPC antennas can be custom-tuned to support a wide range of wireless standards, including:

+ 2.4 GHz / 5 GHz / 6 GHz (Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Zigbee, IoT)
+ GPS / GNSS (Location and Navigation)
+ 4G LTE / 5G Sub-6 / NB-IoT (Cellular and Wide-Area IoT)
